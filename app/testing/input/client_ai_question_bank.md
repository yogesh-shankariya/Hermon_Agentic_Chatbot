# AI Analytics Chatbot Question Bank

Based on the client conversation, the client does not only want a normal lead analytics chatbot.  
They want an AI analyst that can answer business questions like:

- What is going on?
- Where are sales coming from?
- What is working?
- What is misleading?
- What should we do more of?
- Can I trust these numbers?
- What data is behind this metric?

The questions below are grouped by intent and can be used for chatbot testing, prompt evaluation, demo preparation, and skill routing.

## 26. Expected Answer Behavior

For many of these questions, the chatbot should not only return a table.  
The preferred answer format should be:

1. One-line business summary.
2. Key numbers.
3. Short explanation of what changed or why it matters.
4. Data quality/confidence note.
5. Drill-down table only if useful.
6. Suggested next action when the question is diagnostic or recommendation-based.

Example:

Question: Where are my sales coming from last month?

Expected answer style:

Sales last month mainly came from Organic YouTube and Facebook Ads-assisted journeys. Organic YouTube had stronger close rate, while Facebook Ads drove more volume but lower ROAS. Around 14% of sales had incomplete attribution, so the result is directional rather than fully final.

Then show a table like:

| Source | Leads | Booked Calls | Sales | Revenue | ROAS | Close Rate | Data Confidence |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Organic YouTube | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | High |
| Facebook Ads | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | Medium |
| Unknown | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | Low |

## 27. Product Takeaway

The client does not want another dashboard full of numbers.  
They want a fast AI analyst that can:

- answer business questions quickly,
- explain where the numbers came from,
- show what data sits behind the metric,
- warn when data is incomplete,
- compare attribution views,
- identify trends and anomalies,
- recommend what marketing or sales action to take next.

The core positioning should be:

AI Business Insights Chatbot for Leads, Attribution, Funnel, Sales, Revenue, and Recommendations.
