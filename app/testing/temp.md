The biggest funnel leak is around call booking and attendance. Many leads either never book a call, or they book but do not complete the call.

Funnel view:

   ↓

Main leak points:
- Never booked a call: 97 leads
- Booked but did not complete the call: 122 leads
- Completed the call but did not sign: 87 leads
- Signed but not paid: 2 leads

What the evidence shows:
For leads created from 25 Oct 2025 to 24 Apr 2026, there were 515 leads in total.

The largest visible leak is the booked-but-not-completed group: 122 leads, or 23.69% of all leads.
Another 97 leads, or 18.83%, never booked a call.
A further 87 leads, or 16.89%, completed the call but did not sign.
Only 2 leads are stuck after signing, so payment collection after signing does not appear to be the main issue.

| Funnel Point | Leads | % of Leads | What it means |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Booked but did not complete call | 122 | 23.69% | Biggest leak: booked calls are not turning into completed calls |
| Never booked a call | 97 | 18.83% | Many leads are not reaching the call stage |
| Completed call but did not sign | 87 | 16.89% | Sales conversion after the call needs review |
| Paid / converted | 135 | 26.21% | Leads that reached paid conversion |

What this points to:
The first priority should be improving call attendance. After that, the next priority is improving call-to-sign conversion.

Confidence:
High — the pattern is clear across 515 leads, and the largest leak is visibly around booked calls not being completed.

Data quality note:
This is based on leads created in the selected period. Revenue and payment values, if shown, are lifetime outcomes for those leads, not revenue collected during that period.

Recommended next action:
First, review the booked-but-not-completed group: reminders, confirmation messages, show-up process, and follow-up speed after missed calls. Then review completed-but-not-signed leads to understand what is blocking people after the call.
